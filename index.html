About

Hi! I’m a multidisciplinary content creator with a passion for bringing ideas to life through visuals. Whether it’s a brand story, a campaign concept, or a simple moment captured in photos and videos. I’ve been working in the creative field since 2014 and my background spans across graphic design, photography, videography, and social media management. I’ve had the chance to explore creativity from different sides, from the fashion industry, where storytelling meets aesthetics, to the 3D visualization world, where design and technology blend beautifully.

I enjoy crafting both the aesthetic and strategic side of content. Developing concepts, directing shoots, handling post-production, and ensuring each piece supports a brand’s goals. I believe creativity grows through curiosity and continuous learning. Every project is a chance to improve, adapt, create something that truly connects.
